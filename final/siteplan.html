Nauvoo Tech Academy

The site name is NauvooTech.edu. This is a new university called Nauvoo Tech Academy that provides AI-powered education that is cheaper, faster, and better than traditional schools, both in person and online. Shorter web URLs are better, so for the web domain we just use NauvooTech.edu.

Site Purpose

This site will be a landing page for marketing the new school. Here prospective students can get more information about our program, why AI-powered classes are better than traditional universities, and sign up for informational webinars or enroll.

Scenarios

Why should I choose Nauvoo Tech?

How does AI-powered education work?

How much does the program cost?

How can I get more information about the program?

Color Schema

The site uses a black and gold palette drawn from the Nauvoo Tech school colors, with two neutral supporting colors for readability and background contrast.

  • #1A1A1A – Nauvoo Tech Rich Black: Used for the header, footer, and dark card backgrounds.
  • #C9A84C – Nauvoo Tech Gold: Used for heading underline accents, borders, and call-to-action elements. Reflects the gold in the Nauvoo Tech logo.
  • #FFFFFF – White: Used for body text on dark backgrounds and clean content sections.
  • #F5F5F5 – Light Gray: Used for the main page background to provide a subtle neutral canvas that complements the gold accents.

Typography

Two Google Fonts are used throughout the site:

  • Cinzel (serif): Used for the site name, all headings (H1–H3), and navigation links.
  • Lato (sans-serif): Used for all body text, descriptions, button labels, form fields, and supporting content.

The fallback font stack uses Georgia for Cinzel and Arial for Lato, ensuring consistent rendering if Google Fonts are unavailable.
