Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I choose Nauvoo Tech Academy?

Simply put, we help women around the world to transform their life bigger, better, faster, and cheaper than any other program out there.

What differences will I see in AI-powered education?

Amazing differences! We set out specifically to fix all the problems we saw with traditional education. For example:

  • Traditional programs make you wait to start on a specific date. At NTA, you can start immediately.
  • Traditional programs force you to learn on a schedule. If you understand something faster, you have to wait for the class. If you need more time, the class doesn't wait for you. At NTA, you control the schedule and speed of your own learning.
  • Traditional education means waiting for teachers or graders to give you feedback on your assignments. Often, the feedback is generic and not specific to you. At NTA, you instantly receive individual feedback on your strengths and weaknesses for each assignment and test.
  • Traditional schools have a calendar where they schedule the breaks. At NTA, our flexible schedule means you can take a break whenever you need to. You'll never miss an event because you were stuck in class or had an assignment due.
  • Traditional schools teach everyone the same way. The teacher gives one lecture to a class of 50 students. At NTA, we actually start by doing a detailed evaluation of your current knowledge and learning styles. We study how you learn best and adjust our teaching to fit your learning style. We also don't make you re-learn things you already know.
How long is the training program?

The training typically lasts 6 months, if you are studying full time. However, the beauty of AI-powered education is you can move as fast or slow as you need to in every class.

How can NTA be so much cheaper than other schools?

We recognized a number of reasons why education was so expensive. For example, at other schools:

  • You pay for expensive buildings, offices, furniture, utilities, libraries, and maintenance.
  • You pay for an instructor to teach every class.
  • You pay for teaching assistants or graders to grade assignments and tests.

At NTA, we don't make you pay for anything that doesn't increase your skills and knowledge.

How can you offer classes without instructors?

Most instructors teach the same material from the same slides and books over and over again for years. We built each class once and reviewed all material, assignments, and exams against some of the best universities in the world. We also studied what companies are looking for when they hire people. Then we searched for the best ways to explain each concept. Next, each course was reviewed by a skilled instructor and multiple hiring managers to ensure you have the best learning experiences to prepare you for amazing careers. Finally, we thoroughly tested every aspect of every course, assignment, and test to make sure everything will work perfectly for you.

How do I register for classes?

We simplified education by eliminating the need to register and all deadlines. Everyone will take the same classes in the same order. But you progress through each class at your own pace. You may finish a class in two days or you may need two weeks. As soon as you understand the material taught, you immediately start the next course.

What age should I be to enroll?

We welcome women and girls, who have finished their high school or secondary school. Our students range from 16 to 40 to ... it's better not to ask them their age. If a girl is being home-schooled and would like to enroll prior to completing secondary school, please reach out to us to discuss your situation.

Is it really for women only?

Yes, at this time, our focus is on developing women in tech. At some future time, we may consider creating a program for men as well
